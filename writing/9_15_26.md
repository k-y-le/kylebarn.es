another year, yesterday. an ending, a transition, i can feel autumn in the morning air. birthdays have often been tough for me, but i’m learning how to make it work, bit by bit. a few years ago, i started a tradition where i write a long letter to myself on or around my birthday. it’s a chance for me to reflect, process, ask where i’ve been and where i’m going. and it’s a way for me to piece together an archive of the years passing through me. this year, i’m listening to an impulse i have to be a bit more public with my writing — an impulse that will surely find me traversing unexpected paths — and so i’m sharing some excerpts of the letter with you all.

interspersed are pictures from the burial ritual i hosted yesterday. it turned out to be so delightful; i’m warmed by the willingness of my friends to support my strange projects and the care with which everyone stepped into the space. it was just really awesome to be laying in the park covered with who knows what while my friends read poetry and sang songs and whanot.

...perhaps that’s why i’m getting buried today, covered in leaves and sticks and who knows what else in my favorite place to gather: nellie’s lawn, prospect park. a transition, a metamorphosis, a rebirth. reinvention is possible! and often operates in nearly imperceptible ways. perhaps i’ll arise tonight, surrounded by loved ones, with a new name, a new fount of inspiration, or a new way to style my hair. or maybe i’ll be struck by a more subtle and incisive shift. 

not knowing: that’s the fun of it, right? the sense of wild possibility. here i am, again, shaping a container with care and curiosity, stepping into tantalizing unknowns. i’ve been thinking, much, lately, about wisdom. where we get it from, how it shapes us, what it even means to be wise. questing after questions, i seek to become wise. and wisdom arrives through these transitional moments, times of uncertainty, unknowing. shed this, too: the endless search to acquire knowledge, master the world, shape it to your whims. too long has your life been shaped by this consumption framework — the need to ace tests, win battles of the wits, land triumphant atop the masculinist mount of all-seeing eyes. take pleasure, still, in your daily geography quizzes and wonder at cool bug facts. but know that wisdom is shaped not through acquisition, but application. selective application, subjective application, situated application. wisdom is a practice.

in twenty-seven, i hope that i was wise. i hope that i loved, that i cared, that i gathered knowledge, lost knowledge, but most of all continued to learn and grow. here i am, always fitting things in, fitting out — that is, reaching out, just reaching out! trying to get out from under, making space in the weeds, disentangling just to re-tangle in a new way. 

i hope that people will say i was full of spirit, even those who i no longer spend time with. that i showed up for others, especially those without much voice: the creatures, spirits, humans, and other beings too-often overlooked. i strove to be dependable, consistent, to keep my word, to keep trying even when it seems too much. and sometimes it does seem too much, is too much, and i hope i acted out of care even when that was the case...

...on friday, as i biked from brooklyn heights to crown heights, i found myself struck by a premonition. someone in my life will die today, i felt so strongly that i had to pause and take a moment. it wasn’t a tragic feeling, moreso that death visits us all, and this death would be just as it is, as it always would be. i don’t believe in predestination, but i believe in destinations, in travels, in making my way through the muck and the mud, making shapes into the future, watching whirlpools in my wake. i might have just been picking up on the collective mood of 9/11 in new york, or perhaps i was anticipating this day, day of burial and birth and death, of a sort. and sensing into the death always surrounding us, whether we want to acknowledge it or not. i am a death-dealing being, squashing fruit flies in my apartment and eating fruits from afar and making apocalypses via shipping containers, supply chains, single-use plastic. 

what am i seeking in a burial? i think i’m searching for some new perspective. even just in the past week i’ve been feeling the spirit of renewal, descending from the craggy peaks of the rockies to the more gentle ancient spirits of appalachia and back to sea level, where i’ve grown accustomed to the scent of the sea filtered through the microbial bath of the gowanus canal floating from down the block. deaths are opportunities to bask in the transition. peripheries make potent possibilities. so, some wishes for twenty-eight. 

i want this year to be full of long wandering walks, new adventures that shape me and surprise me. i want to dance, to dance! to stretch into my bigness and relish new acoustic terrains. i want to keep making beautiful art with my friends, and to add new collaborations alongside those that keep me going currently. and through it all, i want to feel active and full and beautiful and cherished. i want to feel into the edges of my one wild and precious life while holding loosely enough to it to ride the currents where they want to take me. i keep asking: what if it was easy? to write beautifully and from the heart. to draw to dance to dream. to create things that surprise even me, delight others, ask questions without clinging to answers. i hope for all this and more. i hope to be joined on these journeys.
