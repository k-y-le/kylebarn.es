on friday i try to take a nap, unsuccessfully. i get up and feel as though everything has subtly shifted. i wander the house, walking back and forth seeking something resembling purpose for my day. uncertain, i do one of the only things i know to do in moments like this - i walk; i wander.

among other puzzles, i come across a pipe covered in insulation. somewhere in soho, on my way to eataly to buy overpriced ingredients for an event destined to be canceled, though i didn’t know it yet. fiberglass twists around the pipe, a DNA helix embrace. pink extrudes from the folds like mystery meat, a cursed version of those twist apart crescent roll tubes. did you know that the pink of the fiberglass isn’t even natural? it started because a manufacturer wanted to stand out. you know what they always say, it’s a dog eat dog world in the fiberglass manufacturing industry.

but it’s still a pink day. i make my way to the high line, watching the pinks and oranges of the evening descend. it feels like the last day, ever. wind picking up, state of emergency declared, storm whispers graze our necks. i see a rainbow after no rain, which feels wrong somehow, order of operations gone amok, physics playing practical jokes.

days have colors. seasons, too. this week we stepped into fall, unceremoniously, for me at least, taking out the trash at 9:30pm. what color will this season be? i close my eyes and imagine an iridescent black, oil slick, shining feathers. maybe i’m just listening to my most recent corvid playlist, inspired by an enterprising crow perched next to me at the only le corbusier building in america last week. i was flush with colors, shapes. he was seeking some. we meet in the iridescent black.

but i’m just saying things. seasons and storms humble us, reminder us that control remains an illusion. each week i move files around the internet. i fill myself up and empty out again. seasons change, colors too. a nor’easter arrives and suddenly i’m wondering who i am when i’m not hosting. am i moving too fast? is this really the life i want to live? why do we call it a nor’easter anyways? as usual, i’m mostly questions, no answers. it’s because the winds come from the northeast, duh.

crying at love again. my favorite part of a wedding is when the bride walks out and then i see her, i look over and i see the groom see her, i see everyone else see her, i think about first times and being witnessed and celebrations and culminations and beauty, always beauty. probably i will feel similarly at a gay wedding but i haven’t been to one of those yet.

it rains like today when i’m driving back to boston, with a stop by a river for a drizzling hike. it sometimes seems we’re so used to gray, wet gray, complaining gray. but i think each one is different, today’s skies subtly shifted from last week’s, from the west virginia downpour a few weeks ago. i toss my wilted marigolds to the compost.

in our supersaturated world, the subtleties of color drift away from us. ever since i stopped using the internet so much, i’ve found colors more vibrant, from nor’easter gray to marigold orange. i like to think of my decision to forego social media, to organize against platforms, as not a move away from, but a move toward. toward more presence, more color, more discovery, delight. more knowing myself, my influences. i think it’s more fun, too. every day i encounter new colors, new storms, new pipes oozing pink fiberglass. every day a new world, a future, iridescent black.
